## About

I’m an AI and full-stack software engineer building AI applications, web products, and systems that support business operations. I work across the AI workflow and the application around it, connecting models with data, APIs, and user interfaces.

I’ve worked with **SingularityNET** and **iCog Labs** on systems spanning **multimodal RAG, AI agents, and generative video**. My broader experience includes recommendation systems, analytics tools, payment integrations, and business workflow automation.

I enjoy understanding how a system fits together, making practical engineering decisions, and turning complex requirements into software people can use.

<br>
