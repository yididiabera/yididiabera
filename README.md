### About

I build AI applications, web products, and systems that support business operations. My work spans the AI workflow and the application around it: preparing data, connecting models and APIs, and building the interfaces people use.

I've worked with **SingularityNET** and **iCog Labs** on **multimodal RAG, AI agents, and generative video**. My broader experience includes recommendation systems, customer-feedback analytics, payment integrations, and business workflow automation.

### What I work on

- **AI & retrieval:** LLM integration, multimodal RAG, hybrid search, reranking, and agents that use tools to complete multi-step tasks.
- **Machine learning:** NLP, recommendations, generative models, fine-tuning, and data preparation and evaluation pipelines.
- **Full-stack applications:** Web interfaces, APIs, authentication, permissions, databases, and real-time features.
- **Business systems:** Analytics dashboards, payments, customizations, and automation that reduces repetitive work.
